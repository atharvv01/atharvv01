I have a bad habit: every time my life has a small problem, I build an app for it — money, food, fitness, cravings, wishlists, the lot. Eventually I had too many apps and no way to make sense of them together, so I built **one more app to connect all the others.**

### 🧠 ProjectX — the brain of the operation

[**ProjectX**](https://github.com/atharvv01/ProjectX) is one private dashboard that stitches all my little apps together and answers the questions none of them can alone — like *"I want that thing… can I actually afford it, and if not, how many months away am I?"* It understands that a monthly SIP isn't spendable cash, so the answers are realistic instead of naive arithmetic.

### 🛰️ …and the little apps that feed it

- 💸 [**Paisa**](https://github.com/atharvv01/Paisa) — local-first finance tracker for iOS where every rupee gets a job
- 💳 [**WhereMyCardAt**](https://github.com/atharvv01/WhereMyCardAt) — keeps track of which card did what, and where it's hiding
- 🛒 [**VirtualKart**](https://github.com/atharvv01/VirtualKart) — a wishlist that does the math *before* I do something I'll regret
- 🏋️ [**fitness-intelligence**](https://github.com/atharvv01/fitness-intelligence) — calorie + workout tracking with an AI coach that won't let me skip leg day
- 🍜 [**Tere Gully Main**](https://github.com/atharvv01/TeriGullyMainMobileApp) — map-based food discovery for *cravings*, not whoever paid to rank
- 💡 [**BuildDisShyt**](https://github.com/atharvv01/BuildDisShyt) — catches app ideas by voice before they evaporate in the shower
- ✅ [**ai-todo-planner**](https://github.com/atharvv01/ai-todo-planner) — turns my messy 2 a.m. brain-dumps into an actual prioritized plan
- 🛜 [**PortWatchTower**](https://github.com/atharvv01/PortWatchTower) — desktop app to find out what's squatting on port 3000. Again.

### 🤖 How I actually build

Mostly with AI agents driving the keyboard — lots of code written, lots of code wiped. The graveyard is part of the process; shipping the right small thing matters more than keeping the first draft.
